SUNDER

A round-based tactical FPS in three.js. You lead STRIKE: four of you against four AEGIS defenders. Win the round by eliminating their team or by planting the charge on site A or B and letting it blow. Lose the planter and the charge drops where they fell. First team to 9 rounds takes the match. Money comes from kills and round results — spend it in the buy phase, and remember: a still, crouched shot is a straight one.

  • WASD Move
  • Mouse Look · Click Fire
  • Shift Walk (quiet, steady)
  • Ctrl / C Crouch
  • Space Jump
  • R Reload
  • 1 / 2 Primary / pistol
  • E Hold: plant / defuse
  • B Buy menu
  • RMB Sniper scope
